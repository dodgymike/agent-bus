Base-rate precision of an unranked prose mention is 17% (213 of 1,244 have a real edge) -- 353 unranked pairs is not reviewable; 120 ranked pairs is a queue someone can actually work through.

INTERESTING FINDING, ALSO RECORD IT: of the 363 real edges, 213 (59%) are also mentioned in prose. So prose corroborates most real edges but predicts them poorly in the other direction -- most prose mentions do NOT correspond to a real edge. That asymmetry retroactively validates CONTEXT-SPEC-DEPS's decision to label derived references non-authoritative and render them in a separate section from real relations: had prose been a good predictor, merging the two into one view would have been the honest design. It is not, so the separation stands.
