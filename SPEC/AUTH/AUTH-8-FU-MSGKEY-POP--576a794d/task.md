Enrol records MessagingPublicKey as presented and takes NO proof of possession of the matching private key. service.go:681-684 states this explicitly: the messaging-key check 'cannot stop an enroller registering some OTHER agent's public key as its messaging key, because NO PROOF OF POSSESSION of the messaging private key is taken at enrolment.
