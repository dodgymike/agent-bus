## Description

internal/relay/registry.go keys its peer map CASE-FOLDED but then compares the stored `st.busID` field EXACTLY against the caller-supplied id at every lookup: `Route` (registry.go:495-509, exact check at :506), `PeerBaseURL` (:620-624, exact check at :624), and the same pattern recurs at :301, :314, :386, :527, :548/:551, :585. A caller that folds the id before calling in gets `ok=false` even though the folded id resolves to the right map bucket, because the post-lookup exact comparison then fails.

Why this matters and where it was found: RELAY-19s DeriveJobID wiring (Forwarders outbox job-id derivation) was asked by its implementer-facing doc to fold the peer bus id at the enqueue boundary. The RELAY-19 implementer DECLINED to do that fold, specifically because folding at the caller would make a mixed-case peers RECOVERED jobs unroutable after a restart: Resume reads the persisted job id, re-derives/looks it up through Registry.Route/PeerBaseURL, and a folded id would fail the exact `st.busID` comparison against the peer records original-case spelling while a correctly-cased id would succeed -- an inconsistency that would only surface on replay, not on the happy path. See RELAY-19 task notes (feature-runner report, 2026-08-14) for the full reasoning; the decline was confirmed correct in review because Registry is the single source of peer ids reaching Enqueue (registry.go targets() at :509/:551), so today the double-mint is unreachable from that call site -- but the underlying inconsistency (fold-keyed map, exact-compared field) remains latent and will bite the next caller that does not have RELAY-19s reason to avoid folding.

Fix at the Registry, not at callers: make the comparison consistent with the maps case-folded keying -- either fold `st.busID` too before comparing (and confirm nothing downstream depends on preserving the callers original casing across this boundary), or stop storing/comparing raw `busID` at all and derive the canonical form once at insertion. Whichever direction is chosen, add a test with a mixed-case peer id that exercises Route AND PeerBaseURL AND a resume-style re-lookup after a folded-vs-exact mismatch would previously have diverged.

Do not re-litigate RELAY-19s decline -- it was correct given todays exact-comparison semantics. This task is about making the Registry itself consistent so future callers (including a future DeriveJobID change) do not have to route around it.
