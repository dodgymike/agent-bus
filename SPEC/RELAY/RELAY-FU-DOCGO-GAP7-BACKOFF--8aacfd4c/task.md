P2: a live-traffic correctness/efficiency issue (an honest peer hammering a quota refusal with immediate retries), not a security hole on its own -- but worth noting it compounds RELAY-FU-INGEST-RATELIMIT (e7c66d83, P0, filed today): once a real rate limit/quota exists on the relay ingest path, its refusal shape matters, and a 503 is the wrong shape for the same reason RELAY-FU-BUSPATH-OFFBYONE's 503 is wrong (an honest, correctly-behaving peer retries forever). Real relates relation wired to both.

SCOPE: map idem.ErrAgentQuota and idem.ErrCapacity to a back-off-shaped answer (not a bare 503) in the AcceptRelay gate path.
