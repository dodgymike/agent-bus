## Description

internal/relay/outbox.go's OutboxRecord/relayJob carry Size and ContentSHA256 (outbox.go:409-415,
449-450, validated at :533-562) describing the message BODY, but do not cover Sender, Recipients or
OriginBus. A resumeJob rebuild-integrity check (forward.go, Resume path) can therefore confirm the
body hash of a recovered job but cannot detect a same-body/different-recipients (or different-sender)
substitution of the durable record between write and replay.

Severity is bounded and this is explicitly a DEFENCE-IN-DEPTH gap, not a live vulnerability: it
requires a COMPROMISED LOCAL STORE (an attacker who can already rewrite this bus's own on-disk
outbox records), and even then the RECEIVING bus's signature check still refuses a substituted
message, because Forward (message.go) copies Signature/Sender/OriginBus/Recipients from the
ORIGINAL signed envelope verbatim and the peer verifies against that signature, not against
anything the local outbox record claims. Reported PASS (no P0/P1) by RELAY-19's security gate,
2026-08-14, as finding P2-4.

Fix direction: extend OutboxRecord to also cover Sender/Recipients/OriginBus (or a hash of the full
envelope rather than just the body), so a local-store compromise is caught at rebuild time instead of
relying entirely on the receiving bus's signature check as the only backstop. Add a test that
mutates a persisted record's recipients/sender out from under a live process and asserts Resume
refuses or flags it rather than silently rebuilding and re-offering.

Source: RELAY-19 file-followups brief item 4 (security P2s), 2026-08-14.
