Residual noted by the reviewer on RELAY-24-FU-STOREMSGLOOKUP (c6530638-7cca-4404-bc61-88ca6c2d30b9), explicitly NOT independently verified by the integrator when that task was closed out -- filed here so the distinction (reviewer-asserted vs verified) is not lost.

Reviewer's exact wording (non-blocking residual (a) on that task's review):
"byID's insert and prune are unconditional on the assumption local ids are unique -- if invariant 1 were ever violated upstream, pruning the older twin would remove the survivor's entry (fail-closed, byOrigin has a guard for the analogous shape and byID does not)."

In other words: internal/store's byOrigin index has a guard for the duplicate-origin-id shape (the prune guard is keyed on the LOCAL message id, not Pos, specifically because Append's non-monotone branch can admit duplicate positions -- see store.go's pruneLocked doc and DECISIONS.md 2026-08-15). byID has no equivalent twin-guard: its insert and prune both assume local message ids are unique, which invariant 1 (server-authoritative, never-reused ids) is supposed to guarantee. IF that upstream guarantee were ever violated (a defect elsewhere, not a defect in this package), pruning the older of two same-id messages would delete the byID entry regardless of which copy survives -- the reviewer calls this FAIL-CLOSED (the entry becomes unresolvable rather than resolving to the wrong message), not fail-open, but it is UNVERIFIED: no test exercises a same-local-id duplicate through byID the way TestStoreDuplicateOriginMessageIDs exercises the byOrigin duplicate-origin-id shape.

SCOPE FOR WHOEVER PICKS THIS UP: (1) confirm the fail-closed characterization with a test that deliberately violates the local-id-uniqueness assumption (bypassing whatever normally prevents it) and asserts the outcome; (2) decide whether byID needs a twin-guard symmetric to byOrigin's, or whether fail-closed is an acceptable outcome given invariant 1 is meant to make the precondition impossible upstream; (3) if a guard is added, it must not weaken invariant 1 by treating duplicate local ids as routine -- the correct response to ever observing one is a loud, specific log (invariant 6), not a silent resolution.

P3: reviewer-asserted-fail-closed, not verified, no known live exploit path (invariant 1 is meant to make the precondition impossible) -- but the asymmetry itself (byOrigin guarded, byID not) is real and worth closing or explicitly justifying.
