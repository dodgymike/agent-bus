## Epic description

Research and de-risk the Signal-style double-ratchet requirement, with one governing constraint: WE DO NOT WRITE OUR OWN CRYPTO. Rolling bespoke primitives, bespoke X3DH, or a bespoke ratchet is the single most dangerous thing this project could do -- the failure mode is silent, catastrophic, and undetectable by ordinary tests. This epic's output is evidence and a decision, gating the CRYPTO implementation epic.

RESCOPE, user instruction verbatim (2026-08-02): "ok, let's keep it simple and just use standard message auth/integrity using libsodium. encryption can come later." RATCHET-3's question (do we need full Signal semantics?) is now ANSWERED DIRECTLY BY THE USER: no. RATCHET-1 (deep dive), RATCHET-4 (broadcast under pairwise ratchets), RATCHET-5 (ratchet-state durability) and RATCHET-8 (record-the-decision gate) are superseded -- there is no ratchet, so there is nothing to deep-dive, fan out under, durably checkpoint, or gate. RATCHET-2 (threat model), RATCHET-6 (known-answer tests) and RATCHET-7 (supply-chain review) are KEPT and rescoped to the sign-only design and now sit under invariant 9 (never write your own crypto; always use a well-known, standard, audited library). The bulk of new implementation work lives in the SIGN epic.
