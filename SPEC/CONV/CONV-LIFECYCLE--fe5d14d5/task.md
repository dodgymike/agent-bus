## Description

DECISION TASK for questions the operator did NOT specify. Do not invent answers; produce a ruling
and REPORT IT TO THE OPERATOR.

  1. **Does a conversation END?** Closed explicitly, expired on idleness, or retained forever?
  2. **May a participant LEAVE**, as distinct from being removed by the creator/admin? The operator
     specified only that the creator or an admin may CHANGE the recipient list. Self-departure is a
     different authorization question and was not answered. (Note: if self-leave is allowed, it is
     the only membership change a non-creator may make, which is a deliberate exception to
     CONV-AUTHZ-CREATOR and must be stated as one.)
  3. **What EVICTS a conversation, and what RETAINS it?**

WHY THIS IS NOT COSMETIC: **a conversation that is never closed is unbounded state EVEN FROM HONEST
USE.** This is the honest-traffic twin of the adversarial bound in CONV-PEERQUOTA -- that task
bounds a malicious peer, this one bounds ordinary use. A design where neither exists grows without
limit.

INTERACTS DIRECTLY WITH CONV-SUCCESSION: a creator-only mutation rule plus no expiry produces
conversations that are permanently frozen AND permanently retained. Read that task before ruling.

Definition of done:
  1. docs/conv/LIFECYCLE-DECISION.md carrying, each on its own line:
     `RULING_END: CLOSEABLE|EXPIRING|RETAINED_FOREVER`;
     `RULING_LEAVE: SELF_LEAVE_ALLOWED|REMOVAL_ONLY`;
     `RULING_EVICT: <the eviction/retention rule>`.
  2. An explicit statement of the resulting per-bus state bound under HONEST use.
  3. A DECISIONS.md entry, and the questions reported to the operator.

Parallel-safety: design only.
